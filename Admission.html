Admission Information

Welcome to the Student Hub Admission Portal. We are committed to providing students with a smooth and transparent admission process. Candidates can apply online by filling out the admission form and submitting the required documents before the application deadline.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Must have passed 10+2 or equivalent examination.
  • Minimum 50% aggregate marks from a recognized board.
  • Valid entrance examination score (if applicable).
  • Original documents must be produced during verification.

Required Documents

  • 10th Marksheet
  • 12th Marksheet
  • School Leaving Certificate
  • Passport Size Photographs
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Entrance Exam Scorecard (if applicable)

Admission Process

  1. Fill the online admission form.
  2. Upload all required documents.
  3. Pay the application fee.
  4. Attend document verification.
  5. Receive admission confirmation.
